# Product

## Register

product

## Users

NBA draft fans, scouts, analysts, and prospect watchers who want a quick guessing game built from real scouting-report language. Most sessions should work cleanly on a phone, with desktop staying compact enough to play in one focused view.

## Product Purpose

Guess the Player is a Wordle-like basketball guessing game. It shows one cleaned scouting-report clue at a time, lets the user search the available player pool, and reveals additional clues and hints only after missed guesses. Success means the game feels fast, fair, and replayable without leaking the answer through headshots, names, source metadata, dates, or overly specific draft feedback.

## Brand Personality

Spare, sharp, competitive. The interface should feel like a daily puzzle: simple, squared off, readable, and confident.

## Anti-references

Avoid dashboard density, marketing-site hero sections, decorative shadows, heavy rounding, oversized explanatory copy, visible headshots before the answer is solved, and feedback that gives away exact distances such as pick gaps.

## Design Principles

One clue per beat.

Every hint must feel earned by a guess.

The answer stays hidden until the player is solved or the round ends.

Mobile is the primary surface; desktop should be compact, not sprawling.

Clean the scouting data enough that excerpts feel intentional rather than broken.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast, support keyboard-first guessing, respect reduced-motion preferences, and avoid color-only feedback for correctness or hint status.
